Read-only discovery for The Anchor's Facebook and Instagram schedule, 18 October to 31 December 2026. Nothing was changed while preparing this.

## Verdict

What is scheduled is in good shape:
- The 66 event posts were rewritten today with the new copy engine.

## 2. Recommended plan

### A. Keep the event-order rule, permanently
- **Promotion window:** the planner works out each event's window from the events system. The window opens when the last event of the same type ends. Slots before it are dropped, or refused with the reason. The same rule applies in the create wizard and in the calendar tool (B).
- **Daily check:** a check flags any scheduled post that breaks the rule, for example after an event moves.
- **Proposed definitions:**
  - The "type" is the events system's category.
  - A themed one-off in that category counts as that month's event.
  - A cancelled event counts as the last of its type.

### B. Fill the calendar, every day
- **Posting rule:**
  - One feed post a day on Facebook and on Instagram, each with its story using the matching upright image.
  - At most two a day, on rugby and event days, and never two at the same minute.
- **What fills each day, in priority order:**
  1. The event day (07:00) and the two days before.
  2. Rugby round-ups, 72 hours before kick-off, including the finals once their teams are confirmed.
  3. Build-up posts for each open event window, each with its own job: announce, how it works, meet the host, prizes, eat first, last call.
  4. Food and drink: weekday lunch Tuesday to Friday; Sunday roast on Friday or Saturday; Christmas menu and party bookings through November and December.
  5. The venue: private hire, the garden, the team, and family and dog friendliness, using only facts you confirm.
- **New campaigns now:**
  - Sleigh My Name, posting from 14 November.
  - Christmas Jackpot Cash Bingo, posting from 19 November.
- **December:** filling 3 to 31 December needs your Christmas plans first: what is on from Christmas Eve to New Year's Day, opening hours, menus, prices and how to book. Nothing will be invented.
- **How (the calendar tool):** it uses the same pattern as today's rewrite.
  - It drafts the whole period: each day's post, copy from the new engine, its feed and story images, and its time.
  - It checks every post against the order rule and the facts.
  - You approve the list once. It writes exactly that, only where nothing has changed since, and an undo is kept.

### C. Copy that drives bookings
- **Already done:**
  - Today's 66 rewritten event posts lead with the event's real details and give each post its own job.
  - Each has one clear booking line: "Book now" with the link on Facebook, "link in bio" on Instagram.
  - New posts are written with gpt-5.5.
- **Instagram:** name the event in the booking line ("Book Snowball Showdown through the link in our bio"), because the bio page lists several events.
- **Stories:** add a small booking prompt to the story artwork. On Instagram: "LINK IN BIO TO BOOK". On Facebook: "BOOK AT THE-ANCHOR.PUB".
- **Timing:**
  - Move build-up and food posts from 12:00 to 11:00.
  - Keep event-day posts at 07:00 for now.
  - Judge both after four weeks of per-post links (E).

### D. Link-in-bio fixes
- Refresh the event list every few minutes on a schedule, so no visitor sees a stale list.
- Fix the summer-time bug before 28 March 2027.

### E. Learn what works
- **Per-post links:** give each post its own short link (this needs both apps), so clicks and bookings trace back to the post, its time and its angle.
- **Results per post:** store reach, reactions, comments, shares and link clicks for each post from Meta, so the Analytics page shows them.
- **Lunch post link:** fix the weekly lunch posts' link, which is labelled as Instagram.
- **Review in four weeks:** compare post times, content types, and food against event posts.
